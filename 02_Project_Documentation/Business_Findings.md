# Business Operations Analysis – Key Findings

## 1. Overall Claims Performance

The dataset contains 808,002 total claims.

- Approved: 666,762 (82.52%)
- PUA: 102,154 (12.64%)
- Declined: 27,038 (3.35%)

A remaining difference of 12,048 claims requires further reconciliation.

## 2. Monthly Performance

December 2024 recorded the highest monthly claim volume, with 84,860 claims.

February 2024 recorded the lowest monthly claim volume, with 35,250 claims.

## 3. Account Performance

The top five accounts by claim volume are:

1. EA – 140,412
2. Airbnb – 128,930
3. Procore – 58,153
4. Pinterest – 57,177
5. Prudential – 56,731

These accounts contribute approximately 54.63% of total claims.

## 4. Year-over-Year Comparison

Comparing January–April 2025 against January–April 2024:

- January–April 2024: 174,542 claims.
- January–April 2025: 229,408 claims.
- Increase: Approximately 31.43%.

This indicates an increase in operational claim volume during the comparable period.

## 5. Data Quality Recommendations

The analysis identified duplicate records, inconsistent account naming, and claim reconciliation differences.

Recommended improvements:

- Standardise account naming conventions.
- Review duplicate transactions.
- Reconcile total claims against claim outcome categories.
- Refresh and validate the Excel Data Model.
- Establish regular data quality checks.

## Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates how Excel can be used to monitor operational performance, identify trends, evaluate account-level workloads, and support management reporting.
